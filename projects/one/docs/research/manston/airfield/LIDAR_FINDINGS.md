# Manston: recovered airfield features

Read this alongside `lidar_references.png`, `surface_plan.png` and `airfield_manifest.json`. Coordinates are British National Grid metres; north is up. These are reconstruction decisions supported by surface evidence, not an archaeological excavation or a measured airport survey.

| Feature | Evidence and previous gap | Change in the explorer | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main runway 10/28 | OSM alignment, broad straight LiDAR impression and aerial pavement. The explorer's diagonal terrain crop removed much of the western runway. | Complete 2,750 × 61 m marked runway, with local raw-DTM ground beneath the previously clipped area. | Model dimensions are authored from historical civil references; thresholds and markings are museum reconstruction. |
| Broad historic runway platform | LiDAR/aerial edges show substantially more pavement than the narrow marked runway. The 2018 masterplan labels existing redundant runway pavement. | Approximately 230 m overall paved envelope, offset north of the marked runway; differentiated old concrete and asphalt. | Exact historical edge and resurfacing dates need georeferenced engineering plans. It is part of the broad runway complex, not proof of a second parallel operational runway. |
| Parallel and connecting taxiways | All 23 taxiway source records, corroborated by linear paved impressions and aerial imagery. | Joined, filled surfaces with yellow centre markings and intersections clear of stacked pavement. | Widths are estimates; unnamed links retain descriptive names rather than invented historic designations. |
| Northern Y/T-shaped dispersals | Distinct branching hardstanding shapes in LiDAR and aerial images, represented by mapped apron outlines. | Filled dispersal pads connected to the taxiway network. | Individual aircraft allocation, shelter detail and construction date remain to be established. |
| Central apron | Large paved area visible on aerial imagery but absent from the source apron inventory. | Approximate 71,368 m² quadrilateral before overlap removal, between E633330–633690 / N165735–166025. | Corners are interpreted, not surveyed. Source aerial capture date is unknown. |
| Northeastern elongated paved strip | A separate long hard surface is visible in the aerial/LiDAR comparison and source geometry. | Surface included with its connecting pavement, alongside the other 24 mapped apron areas. | Historic function is unconfirmed. Do not label it as a proven runway, training strip or underground structure. |

The generated inventory accounts for all 49 retrieved aeroway records: one runway, 23 taxiway records, 24 apron records and the airport boundary. This is completeness against that source snapshot and the reviewed surface evidence; it does not establish every lost runway or pavement arrangement from every period.
